Yoga for Better Sleep in Singapore | Healthtinity Featured in Lianhe Zaobao

We're delighted to share that Healthtinity Yoga & Therapy was featured in Lianhe Zaobao (联合早报) on 8 September 2026, as part of a feature exploring ways to improve sleep and relaxation.

In the feature, Healthtinity shared how gentle yoga practices, mindful breathing and relaxation techniques can be incorporated into our daily routine to help the body unwind and prepare for better rest.

Why Can It Be So Difficult to Wind Down?

For many of us, going to bed doesn't necessarily mean our body and mind are ready to rest.

A busy workday, prolonged sitting, screen time, physical tension and an active mind can make it difficult to transition from "doing" to "resting".

This is where a gentle yoga practice can be useful.

Unlike a vigorous workout, yoga before bedtime can focus on slowing down, releasing tension and creating a calmer transition towards sleep.

Gentle Yoga Before Bed

In our Lianhe Zaobao feature, Healthtinity demonstrated several gentle yoga practices that can be performed before bedtime.

The intention is not to push deeply into a stretch or challenge the body. Instead, movements are performed comfortably and with awareness, allowing the body to gradually relax.

A simple evening yoga routine may include gentle stretches and resting positions that encourage you to slow your breathing and release some of the muscular tension accumulated throughout the day.

And you don't necessarily need a long practice.

Even setting aside a few minutes before bed to move, stretch and breathe consciously can be a practical way to establish a consistent wind-down routine.

Breathing Is an Important Part of the Practice

Yoga isn't only about flexibility.

How we breathe during practice is equally important, especially when the purpose of the session is relaxation.

Rather than rushing through movements, a slower practice allows us to pay attention to our breathing and become more aware of how the body feels.

This combination of movement, breathing and relaxation is one reason gentle yoga can fit naturally into an evening routine.

You Don't Have to Be Flexible to Start Yoga

One misconception we often hear is:

"I'm not flexible enough to do yoga."

But flexibility isn't a requirement for beginning yoga.

At Healthtinity, we believe movement should be adapted to the individual. A yoga pose doesn't need to look perfect, and you shouldn't have to force your body into a position simply because someone else can do it.

This is particularly important when practising for relaxation. The objective is to find positions that allow you to feel comfortable, breathe and gradually release tension.

Consistency Matters More Than Perfection

Just like exercise, mobility and strength training, the benefits of a wellness routine come from consistency rather than chasing quick results.

You don't need an elaborate bedtime ritual.

Start with something manageable. Give yourself a few minutes to slow down, move gently and breathe. If it works well for you, make it part of your regular evening routine.

Over time, these small habits can become an important part of looking after both your physical and mental wellbeing.
