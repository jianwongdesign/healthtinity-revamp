1. Introduction

Healthtinity (“Healthtinity”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects the privacy of our customers, members, visitors, instructors, prospective customers and users of our website and booking application. This Privacy Policy explains how Healthtinity collects, uses, discloses, stores, processes and protects personal data in connection with our classes, memberships, programmes, facilities, promotions, website, booking application and other services.

By providing personal data to Healthtinity, registering an account, making a booking, purchasing a service, communicating with us or using our facilities, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with Healthtinity’s Terms and Conditions, booking policies, membership conditions and any additional notice presented when personal data is collected.

2. Meaning of Personal Data

“Personal data” means data, whether true or not, about an individual who can be identified from that data alone or from that data together with other information to which Healthtinity has or is likely to have access.

Depending on your dealings with Healthtinity, personal data may include your name, date of birth, gender, telephone number, email address, residential address, emergency contact details, account credentials, profile photograph, payment information, membership details, attendance records, booking history and communications with us.

3. Personal Data We May Collect

Healthtinity may collect identification and contact information; account and membership information; booking, attendance, cancellation and waitlist records; transaction, billing and payment information; promotional eligibility information; feedback, survey responses and customer-service records; device, browser, IP address, cookie and website-usage information; and photographs, videos or CCTV images where applicable.

Healthtinity may also collect health-related information voluntarily provided by you, including information about injuries, pregnancy, medical conditions, recent surgery, medication, allergies or physical limitations. Such information may be collected to assess your readiness for exercise, assist instructors in managing class participation and support the safety of customers and staff. Healthtinity does not use this information to provide a medical diagnosis.

Payment card details may be collected or processed directly by Healthtinity’s payment service providers. Healthtinity may not receive or retain complete payment-card information when payments are handled by a third-party provider.

4. How We Collect Personal Data

Healthtinity may collect personal data directly from you when you create an account, purchase a package or membership, make or cancel a booking, join a waitlist, complete a form or health declaration, attend a class, participate in a promotion, communicate with us, submit feedback, visit our premises or use our website or booking application.

We may also receive personal data from an account holder who makes a group booking or nominates you under a shareable package; from our instructors, employees and authorised representatives; from booking, payment, marketing and technology providers; from corporate partners or referral partners; from publicly available sources; and from social-media platforms when you interact with Healthtinity through those services.

If you provide another person’s personal data to Healthtinity, you confirm that you have informed that person of the relevant purposes and have the appropriate authority or permission to provide the information.

5. Purposes for Collection, Use and Disclosure

Healthtinity may collect, use and disclose personal data for the following purposes:

To create, verify, administer and secure customer accounts; process purchases, payments, refunds and recurring charges; manage packages, memberships, class credits and expiry dates; process bookings, cancellations, waitlists and attendance; issue booking confirmations, receipts and operational notices; provide customer service and respond to enquiries, requests, feedback and complaints; verify eligibility for promotions, trials, memberships and discounts; prevent duplicate accounts, unauthorised sharing, payment irregularities, fraud and misuse of promotions; and enforce Healthtinity’s Terms and Conditions and studio policies.

Healthtinity may also use personal data to manage class capacity, schedules, instructors and studio operations; monitor customer participation and service usage; improve our classes, facilities, website, booking application and customer experience; conduct research, analytics, surveys and business planning; develop new services, promotions and membership options; train instructors and employees; maintain safety and security; investigate incidents, disputes, injuries, misconduct and suspected unlawful activity; establish, exercise or defend legal claims; maintain business and financial records; satisfy insurance, audit and professional-advice requirements; and protect Healthtinity’s rights, property, customers, employees and operations.

Where appropriate, Healthtinity may use contact information to send information about classes, events, promotions, memberships and services that may interest you. You may unsubscribe from promotional communications using the method stated in the communication or by contacting Healthtinity. Withdrawal from marketing does not prevent Healthtinity from sending essential communications concerning bookings, payments, safety, membership administration, policy changes or service interruptions.

We may use personal data for another purpose that is reasonably related to the original purpose, or after providing an additional notice where appropriate.

6. Consent

Healthtinity may collect, use or disclose personal data with your consent, where consent is deemed to have been given under the Personal Data Protection Act 2012, or where another applicable legal basis or exception permits the collection, use or disclosure.

You may choose not to provide requested information. However, if that information is necessary to create or maintain an account, process payment, confirm a booking, assess safe participation or provide a service, Healthtinity may be unable to complete the transaction or provide some or all of the requested services.

7. Withdrawal of Consent

You may withdraw consent to Healthtinity’s continued collection, use or disclosure of particular personal data by submitting a written request to our Data Protection Officer.

Healthtinity will explain the likely consequences of the withdrawal and will process the request within a reasonable period. Withdrawal does not affect personal data previously collected, used or disclosed, and does not prevent Healthtinity from retaining or processing information where it has another valid basis to do so.

Depending on the information involved, withdrawal may result in the suspension or termination of your account, membership, booking access or ability to use certain services. Withdrawal of consent does not automatically cancel a membership, remove outstanding payment obligations or create an entitlement to a refund.

8. Disclosure of Personal Data

Healthtinity may disclose personal data to its employees, instructors, contractors and authorised representatives who require the information to perform their duties.

We may also disclose personal data to booking-platform providers, cloud-hosting providers, software vendors, payment processors, banks, communications providers, marketing providers, analytics providers, insurers, accountants, auditors, lawyers, professional advisers, landlords, building-management personnel, security providers, emergency services, medical responders, regulators and public authorities.

Personal data may also be disclosed in connection with a proposed or completed sale, transfer, restructuring, financing, merger or acquisition of all or part of Healthtinity’s business or assets. The receiving party may use the information for evaluating or completing the transaction and for continuing the affected business operations.

Healthtinity does not disclose personal data to unrelated parties for their independent purposes unless we have an appropriate basis for doing so.

9. Booking and Payment Platforms

Healthtinity may use third-party platforms to manage customer accounts, bookings, memberships, communications and payments. These providers may collect and process personal data on Healthtinity’s behalf or under their own terms and privacy policies.

Although Healthtinity takes reasonable care when selecting service providers, third-party platforms remain responsible for their own systems and independent data-handling practices. Customers should review the privacy terms presented by the relevant provider when creating an account or completing payment.

10. Cookies and Website Information

Healthtinity’s website and booking services may use cookies, software development kits and similar technologies to enable essential functions, remember preferences, maintain security, analyse traffic, measure promotional performance and improve services.

You may adjust your browser or device settings to restrict certain cookies. Some parts of the website or booking application may not function correctly if essential cookies or similar technologies are disabled.

Third-party analytics, advertising or social-media services may collect technical information when their features are used. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

11. CCTV, Photography and Recordings

Healthtinity may operate CCTV at its premises for safety, security, incident investigation, access control and protection of customers, staff and property. CCTV footage will only be accessed or disclosed for relevant operational, safety, security, investigative or legal purposes.

Healthtinity may conduct authorised photography or filming for promotional, educational, training or operational purposes. Where reasonably practicable, customers will be notified before identifiable promotional photography or filming. Customers may inform Healthtinity if they do not wish to appear in identifiable promotional material.

Photography or recording by customers is governed by Healthtinity’s Terms and Conditions and studio rules.

12. Accuracy of Personal Data

Customers must provide accurate and complete information and update their accounts when their details change. Healthtinity may rely on the latest information provided by the customer and is not responsible for consequences arising from inaccurate, incomplete or outdated information supplied to us.

Healthtinity may take reasonable steps to verify information where it is likely to be used to make a decision affecting the customer or disclosed to another organisation.

13. Protection and Security

Healthtinity takes reasonable administrative, physical and technical measures to protect personal data in its possession or control against unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, alteration, loss, disposal and similar risks.

These measures may include access restrictions, account authentication, staff instructions, confidentiality requirements, software security measures, vendor management and secure disposal practices.

No website, application, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers are responsible for protecting their passwords, devices and account credentials and should notify Healthtinity promptly if unauthorised account activity is suspected.

14. Retention of Personal Data

Healthtinity may retain personal data for as long as it is reasonably needed to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, administer an active or former customer relationship, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain business records and support audit, insurance, tax, accounting, safety and legal requirements.

When personal data is no longer needed for a business or legal purpose, Healthtinity will cease retaining it or remove the means by which it can be associated with a particular individual, where reasonably practicable.

Healthtinity may retain anonymised or aggregated information that no longer identifies an individual for analytics, research, reporting and business-improvement purposes.

15. Overseas Transfers

Healthtinity’s booking, payment, cloud-storage, email, analytics and technology providers may operate servers or personnel outside Singapore. As a result, personal data may be stored, accessed or processed in another jurisdiction.

Where Healthtinity transfers personal data outside Singapore, it will take appropriate steps to ensure that the transferred data receives a standard of protection comparable to the protection provided under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012.

16. Access to Personal Data

You may submit a written request to obtain access to personal data in Healthtinity’s possession or control and information about how that data was used or disclosed during the applicable period.

Healthtinity may request information necessary to verify your identity and clarify the scope of the request. A reasonable fee may be charged for handling an access request, and you will be informed of the estimated fee before the request is processed.

Healthtinity may decline or limit access in circumstances recognised under the Personal Data Protection Act 2012, including where disclosure could reveal another person’s personal data, compromise safety or security, interfere with an investigation or disclose confidential commercial information.

17. Correction of Personal Data

You may ask Healthtinity to correct an error or omission in personal data held about you. Healthtinity will consider the request and make the correction where appropriate.

Healthtinity may decline to alter information that it reasonably considers accurate, is an opinion rather than a factual record, or forms part of a record that should be preserved. Where appropriate, Healthtinity may record the customer’s requested correction together with the original record.

18. Data Incidents

Healthtinity will assess suspected data breaches and take reasonable steps to contain and address them. Where a breach meets the applicable notification criteria, Healthtinity will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission and affected individuals within the applicable timeframes.

Customers must promptly report suspected unauthorised access to their Healthtinity account or any communication that appears to misuse Healthtinity customer information.

19. Children and Minors

Healthtinity’s services are not intended for children below the Studio’s minimum participation age. Where a participant is below 18 years old, Healthtinity may collect information relating to the participant and their parent or legal guardian for identity verification, consent, communication, payment, safety and account-administration purposes.

A parent or legal guardian who provides a minor’s personal data confirms that they have the authority to do so.

20. External Links and Social Media

Healthtinity’s website or communications may contain links to external websites, applications or social-media platforms. Healthtinity does not control the privacy, security or content practices of those external services. Accessing an external service is subject to that provider’s own terms and privacy policy.

Information posted publicly on social media, review platforms or other public channels may be viewed, copied or used by other parties. Customers should avoid publishing sensitive or confidential information through public channels.

21. Business Communications and Do Not Call Requirements

Healthtinity may contact customers using email, telephone calls, text messages, messaging applications, application notifications or social-media channels for service administration and promotional purposes.

Promotional messages sent to Singapore telephone numbers will be managed in accordance with applicable Do Not Call requirements. Customers may request to stop receiving promotional communications, but Healthtinity may continue sending messages necessary to complete transactions, manage accounts, provide requested services, address safety matters or fulfil existing obligations.

22. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Healthtinity may amend this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in its services, technology, operations or data-handling practices. The revised policy will be published on Healthtinity’s website or booking application with an updated effective date.

Customers should review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of Healthtinity’s website, application or services after an updated policy takes effect constitutes acknowledgement of the updated policy. Where additional consent is necessary for a new purpose, Healthtinity will seek that consent separately.

23. Data Protection Officer and Contact Details

Questions, complaints, access requests, correction requests and requests to withdraw consent should be directed to Healthtinity’s Data Protection Officer.
