About Healthtinity · Our story

Where fitness meets healing.

Founded in 2019, Healthtinity was created with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between traditional fitness and rehabilitation. By combining Yoga, Mat Pilates and therapeutic movement, we've helped thousands of people reduce pain, improve mobility and build strength in a safe, supportive environment.

Meet the people behind it

Relieve pain

Programmes for back, neck and shoulder pain, sciatica and posture-related issues, guided by coaches who understand the body.

Restore mobility

Improve your posture, regain your range of motion and move through daily life with ease.

Build strength

Mindful, progressive training for a strong, resilient body at every age and stage.

Dr KaiDoctor of Osteopathy · Founder

Signature programme

Yoga Rehab Therapy.

Healthtinity pioneered one of Singapore's earliest Yoga Rehab Therapy approaches. It integrates Osteopathy, Yoga and Mat Pilates to support the body's natural healing process, relieve pain, restore mobility and improve overall well-being.

  • Chronic neck & back pain, sciatica, scoliosis
  • Sports injuries & post-surgical rehabilitation
  • Postural dysfunction & joint mobility
  • Personalised 1:1 plans that target the root cause
