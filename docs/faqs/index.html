How do I book a class?

All classes must be booked through the Healthtinity booking website or application. Bookings open seven days in advance and close one hour before the class begins. Your place is secured only after the booking is shown as confirmed in your account.

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive at least 10 minutes before the class begins so that you have enough time to check in and prepare. Late entry may be refused for safety reasons and to avoid disrupting the class. A refused late entry will be treated as a late cancellation or no-show.

What is Healthtinity's cancellation policy?

You must cancel your booking through the application at least five hours before the class begins. Cancellations made within five hours of the starting time will be considered late cancellations.

I am a beginner. Which class should I choose?

Beginners are welcome at Healthtinity. Please check the class description and choose a class appropriate for your current experience and fitness level. If you are unsure, contact us before booking and our team can help you select a suitable class.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

Healthtinity provides the standard equipment needed for its regular classes unless the class description states otherwise. Please wear suitable exercise clothing and bring a towel and water bottle. Customers may bring their own mat if they prefer.

Is there a minimum age?

Participants must be at least 16 years old to attend group classes. Participants under 18 must have permission from a parent or legal guardian before purchasing or attending any class.

What happens if a class is cancelled by the studio?

A minimum of three participants is generally needed for a group class to proceed. Where Healthtinity cancels a class before it begins, the class credit used for that booking will ordinarily be returned to your account.
