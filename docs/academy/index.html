Yoga teacher training

Choose a foundation in Hatha and Vinyasa, or build on your experience with multi-style training.

Fees and durations below are from our previous course listings. Contact us to confirm current fees, course arrangements and the next intake.

200-hour Yoga Teacher Training

Hatha & Vinyasa

Build a foundation for teaching Hatha and Vinyasa yoga, with guided practice, alignment, anatomy and lesson planning.

Previously listed fee
S$3,250
Listed duration
10 weeks
View the course outline

Yoga philosophy

History of yoga; the Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita and Patanjali Sutras; Gunas, Koshas, Prana and Doshas; the four paths and eight limbs of yoga.

Practice & alignment

Asana and alignment workshops, meditation, pranayama, adjustments, props and modifications, and the Ashtanga primary series.

Anatomy & teaching theory

Human anatomy, biomechanics and physiology; sequencing, specialised teaching and professional development.

Teaching skills

Vinyasa Krama, lesson planning, class structure, voice and language, and verbal and non-verbal cues.

Assessment

Theory exam, practical exam and assignments.

300-hour Yoga Teacher Training

Multi-style

For yoga instructors and experienced practitioners who want to deepen their practice and teaching. Explore multiple styles, functional anatomy, sequencing and classroom leadership.

Previously listed fee
S$5,400
Listed duration
13 weeks
View the course outline

Yin yoga & restorative practice

Yin and yang principles, more than 20 Yin asanas, modifications and class structure; meridian theory, trigger points and myofascial release.

Develop your practice

Ashtanga primary and secondary series postures, Vinyasa Krama, alignment, guided meditation, Yoga Nidra and kriyas.

Anatomy, philosophy & reflection

Biomechanics, body systems, the Bhagavad Gita, practice and teaching journals, introductory Ayurveda, voice and language, and professional development.

Teaching & class leadership

Teaching ethics, verbal and non-verbal instruction, pedagogy, creative lesson planning, and the use of props, yoga wheels and heated practice spaces.

Assessment

Practical and written assessments.
