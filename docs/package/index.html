How early should I arrive?

Please arrive at least 10 minutes before the class begins so that you have enough time to check in and prepare. Late entry may be refused for safety reasons and to avoid disrupting the class. A refused late entry will be treated as a late cancellation or no-show.

What is Healthtinity's cancellation policy?

You must cancel your booking through the application at least five hours before the class begins. Cancellations made within five hours of the starting time will be considered late cancellations.

I am a beginner. Which class should I choose?

Beginners are welcome at Healthtinity. Please check the class description and choose a class appropriate for your current experience and fitness level. If you are unsure, contact us before booking and our team can help you select a suitable class.
