Our story

Where fitness meets healing.

Healthtinity was founded in 2019 to close the gap between everyday fitness and rehabilitation. We bring Yoga, Mat Pilates and therapeutic movement together, so you can reduce pain, move more freely and build real strength in a safe, supportive space.

We developed one of Singapore's earliest Yoga Rehab Therapy approaches. It brings together Osteopathy, Yoga and Mat Pilates to support how the body heals itself.

Relieve pain

Targeted programmes for back, neck and shoulder pain, guided by coaches who understand the body.

Restore mobility

Improve your posture, regain your range of motion and move through daily life with ease.

Build strength

Mindful, progressive training that builds a strong, resilient body for every age and stage.

FAQ

Good to know.

Still have questions? Message us on WhatsApp.

How do I book a class?

Book through the Healthtinity website or the HT Life app. Bookings open seven days ahead and close one hour before class. Your spot is secured once it shows as confirmed.

I'm a beginner. Which class should I choose?

Beginners are very welcome! Check each class description and pick one that suits your current experience, or message us and we'll recommend one.

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive at least 10 minutes early to check in and settle. For safety, late entry may be refused and counted as a late cancellation.

What's the cancellation policy?

Cancel in the app at least five hours before class begins. Cancellations within five hours count as late cancellations.
